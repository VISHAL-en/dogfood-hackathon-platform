Mathematical Proof & Calibration Model

Dogfood Score Normalization applies chamber-scoped Z-Score Distribution Matching to reconcile differences between severe and lenient evaluator groups.

1. Juror Leniency Standardization (Z_ij)

Z_ij = (X_ij - μ_j) / σ_j

Where X_ij is raw score given by judge j to submission i. μ_j is the judge's empirical cohort mean, and σ_j is their standard deviation.

2. Rescaling to Global Target Baseline (S_norm)

S_norm(i, j) = μ_target + σ_target × clip(Z_ij, -3.0, 3.0)

Target baseline parameters: μ_target = 75.0, σ_target = 12.0.

3. Submission Final Aggregation

Final_Score(i) = (1 / K_i) × ∑ S_norm(i, j)

Where K_i ≥ 3 (Minimum Quorum constraint).
